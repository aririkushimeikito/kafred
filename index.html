Introduction

Lapida Maker and Stone Installer in Rodriguez (Montalban), Rizal

Kafred Granite & Marble Installation Services is a lapida maker and granite and marble installer based in Burgos, Rodriguez (Montalban), Rizal, Philippines.

We engrave and fabricate memorial plaques in black granite, black galaxy granite, blue pearl granite and white marble, and we install the finished stone at the gravesite or on the wall it was made for. Inscriptions, portraits, borders and ornaments are laid out to suit each family's wishes.

A lapida is a lasting tribute. We treat every order with the respect it deserves, from the first conversation about wording and materials to the final installation. See examples of our finished work or read more about how we work.

In loving memory

For the Ones Who Shaped Our Lives

A lapida is more than a marker. It is the place a family returns to on birthdays and anniversaries, on All Souls' Day and on quiet afternoons, to light a candle, lay flowers and say a name out loud. We understand that the stone we make will hold someone's whole life in a few lines: a name, two dates, a photograph, and the words a family chooses to keep.

Those we love do not go away. They walk beside us every day, unseen and unheard, but always near, still loved, still missed, and very dear.

Laging nasa puso. Always in our hearts.

That is why we take our time over every inscription, and why we listen first. Tell us about the person: how they signed their name, the verse they loved, the nickname everyone used. We will help you find the words and set them in granite or marble with the care they deserve.

Words families often choose

  • Forever in our hearts
  • Your memories will remain in our hearts forever
  • Deep in our hearts you will always stay, loved and missed every day
  • Your life was a blessing, your memory a treasure
  • Together in peace, forever in our hearts
  • Mahal ka namin, hanggang sa muli

These are only starting points. A favourite verse, a line from a song, or a few words in your own voice can be engraved just as beautifully. Learn about custom lapida design.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Lapida and Our Services

Short answers to the questions families ask most often before ordering a lapida.

What is a lapida?

A lapida is a memorial plaque or gravestone marker, usually made of granite or marble, engraved with the name, dates and a short message for a loved one. Kafred makes lapida in Rodriguez (Montalban), Rizal.

What materials do you use for lapida?

We work in black granite, black galaxy granite, blue pearl granite and white marble, including veined marble. Lettering can be finished in silver, gold, white or black.

Can a photo be included on the lapida?

Yes. We can add an oval ceramic photo insert or a laser-etched portrait taken from a photograph you provide.

Do you install the lapida?

Yes. Kafred fabricates the stone and installs it, either mounted with brass standoff fittings or set in place at the gravesite.

Where is Kafred located and which areas do you serve?

The shop is at 63B A. Mabini St., Burgos, Rodriguez (Montalban), Rizal, 1860. We serve Rodriguez, Montalban and nearby areas in Rizal; contact us to confirm installation at your location.

How much does a lapida cost?

Each lapida is quoted individually because price depends on the material, size, lettering, portrait work and installation. Call +63 953 084 9511 or message us on Facebook with your details for a quotation.
