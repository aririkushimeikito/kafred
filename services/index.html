Service 01

Lapida Engraving and Lettering

Clean, durable engraving of names, dates, verses and ornaments on granite and marble memorial plaques.

We engrave inscriptions in script and block typefaces and fill them in silver, gold, white or black to suit the stone. Crosses, praying hands, doves, laurel wreaths, roses and scroll borders can be engraved alongside the text.

This is the core of our work as a lapida maker in Rodriguez, Rizal, and the examples on our work page show the range of lettering styles available.

What to tell us when you inquire

  • The full names and dates exactly as they should appear.
  • Any epitaph, verse or dedication line.
  • Preferred lettering colour (silver, gold, white or black) and style (script or block).
  • Ornaments you would like, such as a cross, doves or a floral border.

Service 02

Custom Lapida and Memorial Stone Work

Memorial plaques designed around the person being remembered, including portraits and family layouts.

We prepare layouts for one person, a couple or a whole family, with ceramic photo inserts or laser-etched portraits taken from a photograph you supply. Upright and horizontal formats are both possible, and the design is confirmed with you before engraving begins.

We also make smaller engraved plaques, such as pet memorials, using the same materials and care.

What to tell us when you inquire

  • Whether the plaque is for one person, a couple or a family.
  • A clear photograph if you would like a ceramic insert or etched portrait.
  • The approximate size you need and where it will be placed.
  • Any design you have seen that you would like us to work from.

Service 03

Granite Fabrication and Installation

Black granite, black galaxy granite and blue pearl granite cut, polished, engraved and installed.

Granite takes a deep, lasting engraving and holds a high polish, which is why most of the lapida we make are granite. We fabricate the stone to size, finish the edges, engrave it, and fit it with brass standoff hardware or set it at the gravesite.

If you need granite installation in Rizal beyond memorial plaques, contact us to discuss the project and we will tell you plainly whether it is work we can take on.

What to tell us when you inquire

  • The type of granite you prefer, if you have one in mind.
  • The size and thickness required.
  • Whether the stone will be mounted with fittings or set in place.
  • The installation location and any access considerations.

Service 04

Marble Fabrication and Installation

White and veined marble engraved and installed with respect for the stone's natural character.

Marble gives a bright, classic look and shows black lettering with strong contrast. We engrave marble plaques with bold or script typefaces, add borders and ornaments, and finish the edges cleanly before installation.

Because marble veining varies from piece to piece, we are happy to discuss how the pattern will sit behind the inscription before the work begins.

What to tell us when you inquire

  • The inscription and ornaments you would like.
  • Whether you prefer plain white or veined marble.
  • The size of the plaque and where it will be installed.
  • Any matching stonework already in place at the site.

Pet Memorials and Other Engraved Stonework

Engraved plaques for beloved pets, and inquiries for other granite and marble stonework.

Pet memorial plaques are made in the same granite and marble as our lapida, with a name, dates, a short message and a photo insert if you wish.

For other stonework in granite or marble, such as engraved signage or installation work, please contact us with the details. We will let you know whether it is something we can do rather than promise work we cannot deliver.

  • What the plaque or stonework is for.
  • The text and any photo you would like to include.
  • Approximate size and the material you prefer.
