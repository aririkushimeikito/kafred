Materials & techniques

What You See in These Photographs

Stones: polished black granite, speckled black galaxy granite, blue pearl granite, and plain or veined white marble.

Lettering: engraved script and block typefaces filled in silver, gold, white or black, with crosses, doves, praying hands, laurel wreaths, roses and scroll borders.

Portraits: oval ceramic photo inserts and laser-etched portraits prepared from family photographs.

Installation: brass standoff fittings for wall-mounted plaques, or stones set directly at the gravesite. Read more on the Services page.
